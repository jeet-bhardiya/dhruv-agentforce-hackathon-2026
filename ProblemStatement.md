Market Monitoring Agent
Vikram is a Relationship Manager (RM) for the mass affluent wealth segment in Mumbai. He manages 450 client relationships across the western region. His clients rely heavily on his proactive market recommendations to manage their wealth. To stay effective while on the go, Vikram needs his CRM to act as an intelligent co-pilot on his mobile device, allowing him to research, segment, and advise his entire portfolio at scale.



📋 Current Pain Points
Research Overload: Market analysis requires manual effort across multiple fragmented data sources, making it impossible to provide real-time advice.
Manual Outreach: Sending personalized communications to 450 clients is a manual, error-prone process that is rarely performed in real time.
Information Silos: Client data (family, work, and demographics) is scattered across systems. Vikram often lacks a full 360-degree view before a call, forcing clients to repeat information.
Collaboration Gaps: Liaising with research and back-office teams is broken and slow. In critical financial situations, these delays lead to a loss of customer trust.
🎯 Your Mission
Empower Vikram with an AI-driven Market Monitoring Agent using Agentforce. Your goal is to automate market research, hyper-segment impacted clients, and enable "one-tap" personalized communication to ensure Vikram can provide elite advisory services from anywhere.



💡 Sample Scenarios and Requirements
Real-Time Segmentation: Use Data Cloud to instantly identify which specific clients (e.g., 62 out of 450) are impacted by a specific market event, ensuring communication is only sent to the relevant audience.
AI-Generated Advisory: Use Agentforce to generate personalized market insight messages for impacted clients, significantly reducing the hours spent on manual drafting.
WhatsApp/Channel Preference: Ensure the agent captures and uses the client’s preferred channel (WhatsApp, email, or SMS) for all automated communications.
360-Degree Relationship Brief: Create a Pre-Call Brief feature where the agent summarizes a client’s account, family milestones, and recent work history in a single view.
Automated Activity Logging: Ensure every client response and RM activity is automatically logged back into the CRM, eliminating manual data entry for the sales team.
Slack Collaboration: Enable Vikram to query internal research teams or trigger approval workflows for custom trades directly via a Slack Agent.
🛠️ Technical Requirements
Mandatory Features

To qualify, your solution must incorporate:

Financial Services Cloud (FSC): To manage wealth portfolios, households, and client tasks.
Agentforce: To power the autonomous market monitoring and communication logic.
Data Cloud: To harmonize real-time market data with client portfolio records.
Additional Features

WhatsApp: To deliver personalized advisory messages to the client's preferred mobile channel.
Slack Integration: For RM-to-internal-team collaboration and research queries.
Tableau: To visualize portfolio impact and segmentation for the RM on the go
